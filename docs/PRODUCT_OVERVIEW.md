# Product overview

## The problem

Finding a public signal is not the same as finding a real commercial opportunity. A useful result must connect an observed event, need or business condition to the right company, explain why it may matter, preserve the supporting evidence and make uncertainty visible.

IntentLead is being built around that complete decision unit rather than around a list of scraped contacts.

## The intended outcome

An Opportunity should answer:

1. Which business was identified?
2. What was observed, and where?
3. Which evidence supports the observation?
4. Why could it be relevant to the user's offer and ICP?
5. What is factual, inferred or still unknown?
6. Who is the relevant buyer, when this can be established responsibly?
7. Which business contact is verifiable and appropriate to show?
8. What can a human say without inventing a claim?

The final decision always belongs to the user.

## Current and target scope

| Area | Current public snapshot | Product direction |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery intake | Typed discovery briefs and review foundations | Natural-language intake with explicit human approval |
| Evidence | Provenance-aware contracts and persistence | Broader lawful multi-source discovery |
| Company and Opportunity | Resolution, assessment and review foundations | More complete quality measurement and market coverage |
| Buyer and contact | Domain and storage foundations | Evidence-backed resolution and verification in the workflow |
| Brief or draft | Grounding contracts | Copyable, evidence-linked output for human use |
| External action | No sending | Remains outside the product boundary |

## Product principles

- Evidence before interpretation.
- Facts, inference and uncertainty remain distinct.
- A fluent model response never substitutes for verification.
- Rejection and insufficient evidence are useful outcomes.
- Missing companies, people or contacts are never invented.
- Source and model providers are replaceable implementation details.
- The product supports human research and review; it does not automate outreach.

## Honest status

IntentLead is an active product build. The repository demonstrates a substantial implementation foundation, but it should not be read as a production-readiness or market-validation claim. Live integrations, coverage and outcome quality require separate validation.
